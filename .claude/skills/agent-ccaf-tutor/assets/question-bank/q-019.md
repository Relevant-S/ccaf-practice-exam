## Question

A customer sends: "This is frustrating. I've explained my issue twice and nothing is being resolved. I want to talk to a real person NOW." The agent has not yet called any tools to investigate their account.
What should the agent do?

## Options

- **A)** First call `get_customer` and `lookup_order` to gather account context, then escalate to a human agent.
  - **Rationale:** The customer explicitly asked for a human. The exam guide (T5.2) says to honour that immediately, *without first attempting investigation*. Gathering context first delays the handoff they asked for.
- **B)** Briefly explain what the agent can help with and offer to resolve the issue quickly, escalating only if the customer repeats their request.
  - **Rationale:** Offering to resolve first is the right move when a customer is only frustrated. Here they explicitly demanded a human, so offering alternatives overrides their stated request.
- **C)** Immediately call `escalate_to_human` with the conversation history.
  - **Rationale:** Correct. An explicit request for a human is an escalation trigger, and the exam guide (T5.2) says to honour it immediately without first investigating. Passing the conversation history gives the human agent the context they need.
- **D)** Acknowledge the frustration and ask one targeted question to understand the specific issue before escalating.
  - **Rationale:** Asking another question before escalating delays a handoff the customer explicitly demanded. Acknowledge-and-offer fits frustration *without* an explicit demand for a human.
