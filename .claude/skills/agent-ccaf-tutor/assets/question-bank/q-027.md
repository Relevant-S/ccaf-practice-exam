## Question

After investigating a billing dispute over 25+ turns, you've identified that duplicate charges occurred due to a payment gateway timeout triggering retry logic. The required refund ($847) exceeds your $500 authorization limit. You need to call `escalate_to_human`, and the human agent won't have access to your conversation transcript.
What context should you pass to enable effective resolution?

## Options

- **A)** Your diagnosis and the refund amount only.
  - **Rationale:** Missing customer identifiers and recommended action — the human can't act without them.
- **B)** The complete conversation transcript with all tool results.
  - **Rationale:** Dumping the whole transcript forces the human to wade through 25 turns instead of reading a one-screen brief.
- **C)** The customer's original complaint verbatim plus the tool result excerpts showing duplicate transactions.
  - **Rationale:** Raw artifacts without synthesis force the human to re-do the 25 turns of investigation you just finished.
- **D)** A structured summary: customer ID, root cause, refund amount, and recommended action.
  - **Rationale:** Correct. A structured handoff with identifiers, cause, amount, and recommended action is what a human agent needs to pick up the case instantly without re-investigating.
