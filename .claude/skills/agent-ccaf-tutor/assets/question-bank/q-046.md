## Question

Documents arrive continuously throughout business hours and need structured data extracted. To reduce costs, you want to use the `Message Batches API` (50% discount, up-to-24-hour processing window). Your SLA specifies that extraction results must be available within 30 hours of document arrival with 99.9% reliability.
Which batching strategy is most appropriate?

## Options

- **A)** Submit a single batch at end of day containing all documents from that day
  - **Rationale:** A doc arriving just after morning startup waits all day to be batched plus up to 24 hours to process — well over 30 hours.
- **B)** Submit batches every 4 hours containing documents from that window
  - **Rationale:** Correct. Max 4-hour wait + up to 24-hour batch SLO = 28-hour worst case, leaving a 2-hour cushion under the 30-hour SLA to absorb batch variance and hit 99.9%.
- **C)** Use the real-time API for all documents instead of batch processing
  - **Rationale:** Meets SLA trivially but throws away the 50% cost saving the question asks you to capture.
- **D)** Submit batches every 6 hours containing documents from that window
  - **Rationale:** Worst-case a document waits 6 hours to be batched and then up to 24 hours to process = 30 hours exactly. No safety margin for a 99.9% reliability target.
