## Question

Your schema includes a skills: string[] field. Production monitoring reveals three consistency issues: (1) compound phrases like "Python and SQL" are sometimes kept as one entry, sometimes split; (2) implied but unstated skills occasionally appear in extractions; (3) similar documents produce wildly different array lengths (5-10 vs 40+ entries). Your prompt currently says "Extract all skills mentioned."
What's the most effective improvement?

## Options

- **A)** Enrich the schema to {skill: string, confidence: float, `source_quote`: string}[] to capture extraction metadata.
  - **Rationale:** Useful signal, but doesn't address the underlying inconsistency in how skills are parsed in the first place.
- **B)** Add post-extraction normalization that maps skills to a canonical taxonomy and deduplicates similar entries.
  - **Rationale:** Post-processing can't fix inferred-but-not-mentioned skills or choose the right split for 'Python and SQL' — that decision has to be made at extraction time.
- **C)** Add constraints: "Extract 10-20 skills maximum, one skill per entry, only explicitly named skills."
  - **Rationale:** Hard count caps are arbitrary and can force the model to drop real skills or invent filler to hit the range.
- **D)** Add few-shot examples demonstrating compound phrase handling, explicit mention criteria, and appropriate entry granularity.
  - **Rationale:** Correct. All three issues are about the model's interpretation of what counts as 'a skill.' Few-shot examples teach the pattern concretely — split vs. not-split, mentioned vs. inferred, appropriate granularity.
